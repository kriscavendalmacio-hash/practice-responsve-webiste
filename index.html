History

Epoch

The birth of high-performance design.

1954 – Mercedes-Benz 300 SL "Gullwing": The original blueprint. Featuring fuel injection, a tubular spaceframe, and iconic upward-opening doors, it became the world's fastest production car of its era.

1966 – Lamborghini Miura: Widely celebrated as the world's first true supercar. Designed by Marcello Gandini, its transversely-mounted mid-engine V12 layout set the standard for almost every supercar that followed.

1966 – Ford GT40: Born out of Henry Ford II’s grudge against Enzo Ferrari, this race-car-for-the-road won Le Mans four times consecutively and proved America could build a world-topping machine.
